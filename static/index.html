Calls with Trillionoff
I phone you for free through the Linphone app over Tailscale: at times you choose with a breakdown of your projects, and when something urgent needs you. Answer and talk back: "remind me at three", "send Johan a message saying..."
Call me any time: in Linphone dial trillion and ask for anything, like in the chat. On a call things are done straight away (I repeat back what I heard first). Only payments, refunds and price changes still wait for your OK here.
Set it up (once)
1. On your phone, install Linphone from the Play Store and choose Use a SIP account.
2. User name: your login name. Password: anything. Domain: this PC's Tailscale address (100.x.x.x). Transport: UDP. With Trillion running it signs in and shows connected.
3. In Linphone: Settings, Audio, Codecs: switch on G722 (much clearer sound, so I hear you better). Then Settings, Advanced: switch on Keep alive service / Start at boot. In Android's settings, set Linphone's battery use to Unrestricted, otherwise calls can be missed.
4. Keep Tailscale on, on the phone (Android settings, VPN, Tailscale: Always-on VPN). To call me, save a contact trillion in Linphone, or just dial it. Media encryption must be None (the default). Choose the phone below and press TEST CALL. If Windows asks whether Python may use the network, allow it on private networks.

Watch alerts

Nothing on the watch?
1. On the phone, open the Galaxy Wearable app, then Watch settings, Notifications, App notifications. Choose All apps and switch on Shell (that is the name Android gives alerts sent from the PC).
2. In the same Notifications screen, switch on Show phone notifications on watch while using phone, otherwise the watch stays quiet while the phone screen is on.
3. Check the phone shows the test alert at all. If it does not, the phone may block notifications from Shell: long-press the alert or open Settings, Notifications, and allow Shell.
