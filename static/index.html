Watch alerts
She posts a short notification on your phone and your watch shows it, the same way it shows WhatsApps. Nothing is installed on the watch. Alerts go to your own phone only.
Nothing on the watch?
1. On the phone, open the Galaxy Wearable app, then Watch settings, Notifications, App notifications. Choose All apps and switch on Shell (that is the name Android gives alerts sent from the PC).
2. In the same Notifications screen, switch on Show phone notifications on watch while using phone, otherwise the watch stays quiet while the phone screen is on.
3. Check the phone shows the test alert at all. If it does not, the phone may block notifications from Shell: long-press the alert or open Settings, Notifications, and allow Shell.

My store Gumroad

Connect your Gumroad store
  1. On Gumroad, open Settings, then Advanced, and go to Applications.
  2. Create an application. Any name works, for example Trillion. For the redirect address type http://localhost.
  3. Open the application and press Generate access token. Copy the token.
  4. Paste it here and press Connect.
The token is kept encrypted on this PC under your login only. Other logins cannot see your store.
